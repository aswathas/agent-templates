Building: $ARGUMENTS

**1. Read the project first.** Stack, existing components, stylesheet, theme, brand assets. Do not ask what you can read.

**2. Load the `discovery` skill** and work the seven lines: subject as a verb, the specific audience, what it must not look like, which assets genuinely exist, the stack, the locales shipping now, and every route that applies.

If the request is thin, **state your reading in one line and continue**. Ask only when a missing fact blocks the work.

**3. Write the brief** and show it to me before any code:

```
SUBJECT:    <the one thing, as a verb>
AUDIENCE:   <the specific person>
MUST-NOT:   <competitor or reference to avoid>
REAL:       <assets and content that genuinely exist>
STACK:      <framework, platform, styling, components>
LANGUAGES:  <locales shipping now, RTL yes/no>
ROUTES:     <every method that applies: marketing | app | mobile | canvas | i18n | dataviz | figma>
```

**4. Route and confirm.** Load **every** matching method — they compose. Marketing → `taste-skill`. Product → `app-ui`. Mobile → `mobile-ui`. Canvas → `canvas-ui`. Multi-language → `i18n-rtl`. Charts → `dataviz`. Then build: tokens, then layout, then styling — in that order, never interleaved.

Stop after the brief. I confirm the direction before you spend tokens building.
